How to Build a Regimen

Understanding how to layer and use skincare products is key to building an effective routine. Follow a simple structure to maximise results.

Regimen Basics

Regardless of your skin type or concerns, a consistent routine helps you achieve better results. A regimen can be broken down into three simple steps: prep, treat, and seal.

Step 1: Prep

Preparation is essential to cleanse and prime the skin. This step removes impurities, excess oil, and residue, allowing treatments to work more effectively.

Use gentle cleansers and exfoliants to create a clean base before applying targeted treatments.

Step 2: Treat

Treatments address specific concerns such as acne, dryness, or uneven tone. Apply products from lightest to heaviest to ensure proper absorption.

Water-based formulas should be applied before oils or thicker textures.

Step 3: Seal

The final step locks in hydration and supports the skin barrier. Moisturisers and oils help retain moisture and protect the skin.

Sunscreen should be applied during the day to protect against UV damage.

Types of Products

Skincare products can be grouped based on their function. Cleansers remove impurities, treatments target specific concerns, and moisturisers support hydration and protection.

Choosing the right combination depends on your skin type, concerns, and lifestyle.
